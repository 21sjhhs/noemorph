# Noemorph repository guidance

Use evidence, causal reasoning and verifiable execution records. Do not claim a test, search, subagent or publication occurred without the corresponding result.

For a new research run, load `SKILL.md`; use the five logical roles and the user-confirmed run settings. The maintained protocol is one interpretation role plus four breadth roles, five directions each. Preserve scope, round limits, unselected findings and evidence qualifiers.

For development, packaging, installation, QA or viewing old output, do not start a research run. Read `references/ARCHITECTURE.md` before changing research semantics. Original research sources are retained under `references/01–08`, `references/SOCRATES_SKILL.original.md` and `scripts/run_state.py`; verify them with `scripts/check_upstream.py`.

Read `docs/development.md` for checks. Never include private run directories, credentials, personal paths or unreviewed third-party assets in a release. The bundled HTML viewer and settings card are local tools, not a provider-specific plugin registration or an automatic model-execution API.
