Hidden arguments to preserve: who defines acceptable coordination; which dependencies matter; whether recovery requires the original task; whether maintaining agreement can mask a changed objective; whether measured improvement would occur without intervention; whether the team was coordinated before disruption. Failures to recover and the dissolution of a team remain possible outcomes.

For round-one operator comparability only, P0 is provisionally operationalized as **regaining a mutually acceptable arrangement of interdependent action after impairment, allowing either restoration or reconfiguration and recording which occurs**. A case without prior impairment does not satisfy P0. A changed subject must still be organizational, non-coercive and decentralized in the relevant actions. This working definition is revisable after evidence review, uniformly for all roles.
